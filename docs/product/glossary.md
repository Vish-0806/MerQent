# MerQent — Domain Glossary & Terminology

## 1. Private Equity & M&A Terms
- **PE Sponsor (Private Equity Firm)**: Financial investment firm deploying institutional capital to acquire controlling stakes in operating companies.
- **Platform Company**: The initial, primary acquisition made by a PE firm, onto which subsequent smaller acquisitions ("bolt-ons") are integrated.
- **Add-on / Bolt-on Acquisition**: A smaller company acquired and operationally folded into an existing platform company to achieve revenue or cost synergies.
- **Carve-out / Divestiture**: The operational separation of a business unit or subsidiary from a parent corporation to create a standalone company.
- **TSA (Transition Services Agreement)**: A formal contract where the selling parent company continues to provide shared back-office services (e.g., IT, payroll, ERP) to the carved-out company for a transitional period (typically 6–18 months).
- **Day 1 Readiness**: The operational milestone on transaction close ensuring business continuity (employees paid, supply chain active, cash collection operational).
- **100-Day Plan**: The tactical roadmap executed immediately post-closing to deliver quick-win operational synergies and establish governance.

## 2. PMI Systems & Functional Terms
- **PMI (Post-Merger Integration)**: The process of unifying two or more organizations, their cultures, operational workflows, and software systems following an acquisition.
- **Chart of Accounts (COA) Harmonization**: Unifying the accounting ledger codes and financial structures across merged entities into a single reporting standard.
- **ERP Cutover**: The critical phase where transactional operations transition from legacy ERP systems to a consolidated target enterprise system (e.g. SAP S/4HANA or NetSuite).
- **EBITDA Synergies**: Cost reductions or revenue increases anticipated as a result of operational integration.

## 3. MerQent Technical & Platform Terms
- **Hybrid AI Matching**: Multi-stage candidate ranking combining deterministic relational filtering (availability, taxonomy) with dense vector semantic search and multi-factor weighted scoring.
- **Factual Grounding**: Architecture constraint guaranteeing that all AI match explanations reference verifiable database entities rather than model hallucinations.
- **Verification Tier**: Trust level assigned to a consultant profile based on objective documentation (Identity, Past Engagements, Executive References).
- **Shortlist**: A curated collection of candidate profiles created by a PE deal lead for a specific PMI project.
- **Connection**: A formal two-way mutual agreement unlocking direct messaging and scheduling between a PE firm and a consultant.
