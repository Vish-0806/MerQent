# Phase 1.18 Completion Report — Administration Architecture

## Objective
Design the complete administration control plane architecture for MerQent. Formulate role segregation (Separation of Duties between `PLATFORM_ADMIN` and `VERIFICATION_REVIEWER`), verification queues, taxonomy governance, user/firm moderation, AI match and hallucination audit tools, and immutable audit log inspection.

## Work Completed
1. Formulated complete administration architecture in `docs/architecture/18-admin-architecture.md`:
   - Established strict Separation of Duties: `VERIFICATION_REVIEWER` isolated to trust queues with zero access to private messages or system configurations; `PLATFORM_ADMIN` governed by break-glass audit logs.
   - Designed 5 functional administrative modules: Verification Review Queue, Taxonomy Governance Engine, AI Match & Calibration Center, User & Firm Moderation, and Tamper-Evident System Audit Trail.
   - Formalized taxonomy curation capabilities across all 6 PMI categories with soft-deprecation (`isActive = false`) and hierarchical relationships.
   - Engineered AI low-confidence audit center for inspecting matches with $C < 60\%$ and tracking hallucination anomalies flagged by the grounding interceptor.
   - Enforced administrative security controls: mandatory MFA for admin roles, 30-minute idle session timeouts, and IP subnet allowlisting readiness.

## Decisions Made
- **Separation of Duties:** Trust review staff cannot alter system configurations or access billing/audit logs, minimizing insider threat exposure.
- **Break-Glass Audit for Messages:** Administrators cannot read private user conversations without triggering a formal break-glass audit entry with explicit ticket justification.
- **Soft-Deprecation of Taxonomies:** Obsolete ERPs or terms cannot be hard-deleted from the database; they are deactivated, preserving historical project integrity.
